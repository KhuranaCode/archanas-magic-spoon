FAQ

Honest answers

Is this the shop's official website?+

No. This is a concept demo built to show what a website for @archanasmagicspoon could look and feel like. The shop has not reviewed or approved it.

Are these real products and prices?+

No. Every photo is an illustrative sample and every price is marked as a sample. The shop's real menu and rates would replace them before anything goes live.

Can I order or enquire from this site?+

Not from this demo. The cake studio shows how an enquiry flow could work, but it sends and stores nothing - that is intentional.

Are the business details on the About page confirmed?+

There are none yet - deliberately. The Instagram handle is the one this demo was requested for; the address, hours and phone number are unconfirmed, so the demo shows placeholders instead of guesses.
